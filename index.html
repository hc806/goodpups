How to play
The rules
  • One poodle in every colored yard.
  • One poodle in every row.
  • One poodle in every column.
  • No two poodles may touch — not even corner to corner.

The controls
  • Tap a square to mark it ✕ — "no poodle here".
  • Tap again to clear the mark.
  • Double tap to place a poodle.
  • Drag to sweep ✕ across squares. Start on a blank square to add them, start on a ✕ to rub them out.

Good to know
  • Every puzzle can be finished by reasoning alone. You should never need to guess.
  • A wrong poodle costs one life. You get three.
  • Hints are limited — they refill each day, and every 5 levels cleared earns one back.
